Research

My research focuses on building secure, efficient, and fault-tolerant communication systems across emerging network technologies. I work on the Internet of Things, wireless body area networks, AI/ML for Systems, software-defined and mobile ad-hoc networks, and develop privacy-preserving protocols and blockchain-based applications for tamper-proof digital evidence management.

Wireless Body Area Networks (WBANs)

Wireless Body Area Networks are specialized sensor networks designed for continuous health monitoring through wearable or implantable devices. My research targets the design of lightweight and secure communication protocols that ensure medical data is transmitted efficiently without compromising patient privacy or system reliability. The ultimate goal is to support next-generation healthcare applications by enabling safe, real-time monitoring of physiological signals in resource-limited environments.

Internet of Things (IoT)

The Internet of Things connects everyday objects—sensors, devices, and systems—into a unified digital ecosystem. My research in IoT focuses on developing secure and lightweight frameworks that enable reliable communication between resource-constrained devices while protecting sensitive data. By addressing challenges such as scalability, interoperability, and privacy, my work aims to build IoT solutions that are both efficient and trustworthy for applications ranging from smart cities to industrial automation..

Blockchain Applications

Leveraging blockchain technology for tamper-proof and auditable evidence management, enhancing trust, transparency, and accountability in sensitive digital ecosystems.

Ad-hoc Networks

Designing adaptive and programmable network architectures that support scalability, efficient routing, and fault tolerance in dynamic settings.

AI/ML for Systems

Research focuses on applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to optimize and enhance computer systems. This includes designing intelligent algorithms for resource management, fault detection, network optimization, and predictive maintenance in distributed and cloud environments. By combining learning-driven methods with traditional system design, the approach improves efficiency, reliability, and scalability while reducing operational overhead. Applications span IoT, wireless networks, blockchain infrastructures, and mobile systems, enabling adaptive, real-time decision-making in dynamic environments.

Environmental Pollution

Research focuses on developing sensor-based monitoring and data-driven solutions for detecting and mitigating environmental pollution. This includes designing IoT-enabled air and water quality monitoring systems, applying machine learning for pollutant prediction, and exploring smart strategies for sustainable urban and industrial environments. The work aims to enable real-time monitoring, early warning, and data-informed decision-making for environmental protection.
