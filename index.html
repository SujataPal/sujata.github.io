Research

Overview — I investigate building practical, secure distributed systems. Current thrusts:

Fault-tolerant Systems

Designing atomic broadcast primitives and replication techniques that scale in adversarial conditions.

Blockchain & Privacy

Applying blockchain for tamper-evident digital evidence and exploring zero-knowledge proofs for privacy-preserving verification.
